# 🛡️ SHEVANTA AI

### Your Safety. Your Strength. Your Freedom.

SHEVANTA AI is a women’s personal safety web application designed to help users prepare for journeys, manage trusted contacts, organize personal safety plans, and explore emergency support features through a modern, responsive dashboard.

> **Project status:** Prototype in development. Emergency alerts, live location tracking, and real-time notifications are not yet implemented.

## ✨ Features

- **Personal Safety Dashboard** — A centralized interface for safety tools and activities.
- **SOS Demonstration** — Explore a proposed emergency support workflow without sending real alerts.
- **Trusted Contacts** — Add and manage people you choose to trust.
- **Journey Check-in** — Configure a demonstration check-in duration.
- **Journey Planning** — Follow a practical checklist before travelling.
- **Incident Notes** — Draft notes about incidents for personal reference.
- **Safety Readiness Checklist** — Review important personal safety preparations.
- **Responsive Interface** — Designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **Privacy-focused Design** — Intended to give users control over their personal safety information.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Make personal safety planning easier and more accessible.
- Provide a simple way to organize trusted contacts.
- Encourage safer journey planning and check-in habits.
- Explore how artificial intelligence can support explainable safety recommendations.
- Develop a foundation for future location sharing and consent-based emergency notifications.
